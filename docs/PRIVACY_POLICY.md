# Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 20, 2026

Welcome to **Arrow Escape** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed when you download, install, and play our mobile game application **Arrow Escape**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
Arrow Escape operates with an **offline-first** design. The following gameplay data is stored locally on your device using encrypted or system-managed local storage:
- Current unlocked level and stage progress.
- Stars earned per stage.
- In-game coin balance and unlocked cosmetic skins.
- Audio, haptic feedback, and theme preferences.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
When you connect to the internet, our game and third-party service providers may automatically collect certain diagnostic and operational information:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
- **Anonymous Game Progress & High Scores:** High scores in Timed Rush and total star counts for global and weekly leaderboard rankings.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS) used strictly for non-personalized or personalized advertising compliance in accordance with platform policies.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Escape is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone).
- We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
- If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security

- **Local Data:** Retained on your device until the application is uninstalled or application data is cleared.
- **Cloud/Leaderboard Data:** Retained for leaderboard aggregation and periodically purged for historical rankings.
- We implement industry-standard technical measures to secure any data processed against unauthorized access or alteration.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Under GDPR, CCPA, and applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to:
- Access the data associated with your guest or player profile.
- Request the complete deletion of your scores and cloud profile records.
- Opt out of personalized advertising via your device operating system settings:
  - *Android:* Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Delete advertising ID).
  - *iOS:* Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Ask App not to Track.

To request deletion of your player data from our backend, you may use the "Reset Progress" option in Settings or email us at:
**support@antigravitygames.io**

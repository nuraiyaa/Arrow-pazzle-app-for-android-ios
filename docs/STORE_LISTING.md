```text
Welcome to Arrow Escape — the ultimate minimalist logic puzzle game that challenges your mind and spatial awareness!

Are you ready to test your brain? Tap the arrows and guide them off the board without colliding into obstacles or other arrows. Every move counts!

🧩 GAMEPLAY FEATURES:

• 100% SOLVABLE PUZZLES:
Every level is procedurally verified using reverse-play backtracking algorithms. No unsolvable boards, ever.

1. RELAXED JOURNEY: Play at your own pace with Easy (5x5), Hard (6x6), and Master (8x8) difficulty tiers.
2. OBSTACLE CHALLENGE: Navigate larger grids with immovable stone blockers and tricky bottlenecks.
3. TIMED RUSH: Beat the 60-second countdown! Chain quick escapes for time bonuses and combo score multipliers.

• CUSTOM ARROW SKINS & THEMES:
Unlock and equip stunning cosmetic skins — from Classic Obsidian and Cyber Neon to Golden Royal, Emerald Matrix, Solar Flare, and Void Amethyst.

No WiFi? No internet? No problem! Play all unlocked levels anytime, anywhere without an internet connection.

• CLOUD SYNC & LEADERBOARDS:
Connect online to sync your progress seamlessly across devices and climb both the Weekly League and All-Time Global Leaderboards.

• SATISFYING HAPTICS & SOUND:
Feel tactile feedback with every arrow escape, collision, and victory.

• BALANCED & FAIR:
Zero pay-to-win mechanics. Fair reward economy with daily bonuses, streak rewards, and optional reward ads.

Download Arrow Escape today and experience the most satisfying puzzle escape maze on mobile!
```
